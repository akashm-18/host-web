1. About PAKKA

PAKKA is a software service that enables merchants to send automated WhatsApp order confirmation messages to customers who have placed Cash on Delivery (COD) orders on their Shopify store. The service is built on the official Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API. 3. Service Description

PAKKA provides the following core functionality:

  • Detection of new COD orders placed on a merchant's Shopify store via Shopify webhooks;
  • Sending a WhatsApp template message to the customer's phone number via the Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API, requesting order confirmation or cancellation;
  • Reading the customer's reply and automatically updating the order status in the merchant's Shopify store accordingly.

PAKKA is an independent software product. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a certified partner of Meta Platforms, Inc. or Shopify Inc.
