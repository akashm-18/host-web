How PAKKA Works

Three steps. Zero manual effort. Fewer fake orders reaching your fulfilment team.

1

Customer Places a COD Order

A customer checks out from your Shopify store using Cash on Delivery as the payment method. PAKKA detects the new order automatically through Shopify webhooks.

2

WhatsApp Confirmation Sent

Within seconds, PAKKA sends a structured WhatsApp message to the customer's phone number via the official Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API, asking them to confirm or cancel the order.

3

Order Confirmed or Cancelled

The customer replies with one tap. PAKKA reads the response and updates the order status in your Shopify store — confirmed orders move forward, cancelled ones are flagged or cancelled automatically.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions

What is PAKKA?

PAKKA is a WhatsApp-based Cash on Delivery order confirmation tool for Indian Shopify and D2C merchants. When a customer places a COD order, PAKKA automatically sends them a WhatsApp message asking them to confirm or cancel. Confirmed orders proceed; cancelled or unresponsive orders are flagged, saving you shipping costs and RTO losses.

Does PAKKA use the official WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. PAKKA is built on the Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API — the official, direct channel provided by Meta for businesses. Messages are sent through approved template messages, which means high deliverability and full compliance with WhatsApp's messaging policies. We do not use unofficial or third-party WhatsApp automation tools.

Is my customers' data safe?

Yes. PAKKA collects only the data needed to send order confirmation messages — the customer's name, phone number, and order details. We never sell personal data. Data handling is governed by our Privacy Policy, and we comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Which platforms does PAKKA support?

PAKKA currently integrates with Shopify. If you run a D2C store on a different platform, contact us at hello@pakkaapp.app to discuss your requirements.

How do I get started?

Get in touch through our Contact page or email us at hello@pakkaapp.app. We will walk you through the onboarding process, connect your Shopify store, and set up your WhatsApp messaging templates.
