Our Story

Why PAKKA Exists

Cash on Delivery is the dominant payment method for e-commerce in India. While it lowers the barrier to purchase, it also opens the door to fake orders, prank checkouts, and accidental purchases — all of which result in wasted packaging, shipping costs, and the dreaded RTO (Return to Origin).

PAKKA was built to solve this specific problem. By sending a WhatsApp confirmation message the moment a COD order is placed, we give merchants a fast, low-effort way to verify customer intent before fulfilment begins. The result: fewer bad orders, lower RTO rates, and more confident shipping decisions.

📲

PAKKA uses the official Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API to send and receive messages — the same official channel used by enterprises worldwide. There are no grey routes, no unofficial bots, and no risk to your WhatsApp number.
